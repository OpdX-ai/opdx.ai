OPDX.AI — Landing Page Redesign Brief (Clinic/OPD Platform)

Owner: PM → For: Software Architect & UI/UX Designer
Status: Countdown + Email capture already implemented. This brief focuses on CONTENT, IA, COMPONENTS, and VISUAL REDESIGN for a clinic/OPD doctor platform.

0) North Star
We’re building a platform that streamlines clinic operations for OPD doctors: appointments, queue, OPD notes, prescriptions, billing, and follow-ups. The landing page must speak to single- and multi-doctor clinics in India (front-desk heavy), be fast, trustworthy, and conversion-oriented.

1) Primary Goals
- Convey value to clinic owners and doctors in 10 seconds.
- Drive waitlist sign-ups (existing email form) and demo requests (secondary CTA).
- Be India-first: UPI flows, WhatsApp reminders, GST invoicing, DPDP-aware privacy stance.
- Keep it light: quirky but professional. Avoid stock-photo clichés; prefer clean, abstract visuals.

2) Brand Voice & Quirky Message (choose 1 headline + 1 subhead)
Headline options (quirky, doctor-aware):
- “Less waiting, more healing. Your OPD deserves a brain upgrade.”
- “We cured appointment chaos. Side effects include happier front desks.”
- “Clipboard to cloud—without the drama.”
- “Your queue will feel shorter. Your day will feel longer.”
- “Practice medicine, not spreadsheets.”

Subheadline options (benefit-first):
- “OPDX.AI streamlines appointments, queue, OPD notes, prescriptions, and payments—built for Indian clinics.”
- “Powerful clinic operations in one clean console: bookings, tokens, Rx, UPI billing, WhatsApp reminders.”
- “Run your OPD like clockwork—bookings, visits, prescriptions, and GST bills, all in one place.”

Hero micro-copy (supporting line near CTAs):
- “HIPAA-lite mindset, DPDP-aware. Your data, your clinic.”
- “Install-free for patients. PWA-ready portal.”

3) IA (Information Architecture)
Above the fold (keep countdown + email form intact):
- Logo/Wordmark (temporary acceptable)
- Headline + Subheadline (from #2)
- Countdown timer (already built)
- Primary CTA: “Join the Waitlist” (existing) + Secondary CTA: “See a 2‑minute demo” (links to hosted video later; placeholder now)

Below the fold sections (order intentional):
A. “Who it’s for”
- Copy: Single-doctor clinics • Multi-doctor polyclinics • Specialty OPDs (pediatrics, gynae, ortho, dental GP) • Front-desk teams
- 3 small cards with micro-benfits per persona.

B. “What you get” (Feature value grid: 2 columns × 4–6 items)
- Appointments & token queue — “Cut no-shows, keep the flow.”
- OPD notes & prescriptions — “Templates + dose shorthand like 1‑0‑1 ×5d.”
- Patient 360 — “History, vitals, allergies in one view.”
- UPI/Cards billing with GST — “Receipts that your CA won’t frown at.”
- WhatsApp reminders — “Reduce misses, increase follow-ups.”
- Reports — “Daily settlements, referral tracking, GST summary.”

C. “How it works” (3 steps, simple icons)
1) “Set up your clinic” → hours, fee, staff.
2) “Run your day” → bookings, queue, visits, Rx, payments.
3) “Close and grow” → settlements, reminders, insights.

D. “Trust, Privacy & Compliance” (India-first)
- DPDP-aligned practices: explicit consent, purpose-limited use, export/delete on request.
- Data security: encryption at rest, TLS in transit, role-based access, audit logs.
- Uptime & reliability: Cloudflare edge, R2 object storage, backups.

E. “In the clinic” (visual story strip)
- Small screenshots/illustrations placeholders: Calendar & Queue, Visit Sheet, Rx PDF, UPI payment, WhatsApp reminder preview.

F. “Pricing teaser” (no hard prices yet)
- “Pilot clinics get early-bird pricing.”
- “Simple per-doctor plan, volume-friendly.”
- “Transparent. No lock-in.” (Link to mailto: hello@opdx.ai)

G. “FAQ” (starter list; expandable accordions)
- Is this ABDM/NDHM-ready? → “We’re adapter-ready; integration roadmap is public.”
- Can I use it without changing my phone number? → “Yes, WhatsApp via BSP templates.”
- How hard is migration? → “CSV import for patients; we’ll help.”
- Where is data stored? → “On the cloud with encryption; you own your data.”

H. Footer
- Contact • Privacy • Terms • Minimal social links
- Clinic-friendly footer line: “© 2025 OPDX.AI — Built for Indian clinics.”

4) Visual System
- Palette (professional with a calm clinical base):
  Primary: #2D6CDF (clinic blue) | Mint: #22CC88 | Ink: #0D1117 | Paper: #F7FAFF | Accents: #F59E0B (alerts), #EF4444 (critical).
- Typography: Space Grotesk (headlines), Inter (body); minimum size 16px; fluid scaling.
- Imagery: Prefer light, abstract UI shapes and subtle line illustrations over literal people photos.
- Motion: Micro only (fade/slide 120–160ms). Respect reduced-motion.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA, contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible focus, full keyboard nav.

9) Performance & Accessibility Requirements
- Mobile Lighthouse ≥ 90 across categories.
- ≤ 80KB JS on landing; images AVIF/SVG; width/height set; lazy load below the fold.
- Keyboard-only nav end-to-end; focus visible; skip-to-content link.
- Announce FAQ open/close states to screen readers.

10) Acceptance Criteria (for redesign)
- Above-the-fold communicates core value in ≤ 10 seconds.
- 2 clear CTAs: Waitlist (existing) + Demo (placeholder link).
- Feature grid + How-it-works + Trust strip render cleanly on mobile.
- ScreenshotRail accepts 4–6 static images; safe fallbacks when empty.
- JSON config (see #7) can swap all copy without code changes.
- OG image endpoint updated for clinic visuals.
- Footer includes Privacy/Terms/Contact.

11) Optional A/B Test Ideas (copy-only)

- Persona emphasis: Doctor-first vs Owner-first.
- Secondary CTA: “Book a call” vs “See a 2‑minute demo”.
